My name is **Jeanne Salle**, I'm a first-year PhD student in the **AI Safety and Alignment group** at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, advised by [Maksym Andriushchenko](https://www.andriushchenko.me/) and [Sahar Abdelnabi](https://s-abdelnabi.github.io/). Before that, I participated in the [MATS program](https://www.matsprogram.org/) in Berkeley and London. I'm interested in doing research on better understanding and mitigating risks from increasingly capable AI systems. 

I discovered AI safety during my Master's and volunteered at the [Centre pour la Sécurité de l'IA](https://cesia.org/en/), a French think-tank dedicated to informing policymakers about AI risks. I organized events and weekly discussion groups, taught the AI safety course in [ENS Ulm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_normale_sup%C3%A9rieure_(Paris)) and contributed to the [AI Safety Atlas](https://ai-safety-atlas.com/) (a textbook distilling AI safety research). I'm now facilitating the [AI Safety, Ethics & Society's course](https://www.aisafetybook.com/).

I'm interested in anything AI safety-related: technical research, AI governance, regulations, forecasting/futures studies, how to communicate about risks, etc. Recently, I've also enjoyed studying geopolitics and philosophy.
